Critical issues detected
🔴 30 blog posts live at root / instead of under /blog/
URLs like /boost-driver-accountability-with-fuel-management-systems/ don't inherit SEO authority from the /blog/ hub. They should live at /blog/post-name/ to build a coherent hierarchy and link equity.
🟡 5 case studies are disconnected from /case-studies/
Individual case study pages sit at root, orphaned from the /case-studies/ index. Also, /our-work/ may be duplicating that section without a clear differentiation.
🟡 Typo in section URL: /indsutries/ instead of /industries/
This URL is in the sitemap and likely indexed by Google with the error. It affects crawlability and site credibility.
🟡 Content hub fragmented across 3+ separate sections
/blog/, /petro-talk/, /fuel-management-resource-center/ and /technical-resources-center/ split topical authority with no clear hierarchy between them.
🟡 Conversion landing pages mixed into the main sitemap
/email-verified/, /prequalify-survey/, /calendar-booking-cold-email/ provide no SEO value and waste crawl budget. They should have a noindex tag and be removed from the sitemap.
✅ The /job/ section is the only one with clean hierarchy
All job postings correctly live under /job/. This is the model that should be replicated for blog posts and case studies.
